Ask your own assistant with @, review its draft, send it as you

You type @, pick your assistant, and say what you want. It works where only you can see it and gives you a draft. If you press Send, the message goes out from you. If you don't, nobody ever knows you asked.

In these screens the person is Meera and she named her assistant Tara. Her colleague Karthik named his Max. "Ayati" is only the name an assistant has until its person changes it.

The whole idea in five moments

  1. 1. You ask@Tara and what you want, in any conversation
  2. 2. Tara worksReads the conversation and your data. Only you see it.
  3. 3. Draft cardThe reply, and what it was based on. Only you see it.
  4. 4. You decideSend, edit, ask for a change, or discard
  5. 5. Message from youOthers see an ordinary message with your name

The purple steps are private. Nothing in them reaches the channel or the other person.

Every person has their own assistant, with their own name

The design never depends on the name. Each person sees only their own assistant in the @ list, always first, always marked "Your assistant".

  • The mention holds an ID, not a name. Cuckoo already stores mentions this way. Renaming Tara to anything else changes nothing.
  • You can't reach someone else's assistant. Max is not in Meera's list. If she types "@Max" by hand it is ordinary text.
  • You don't have to remember the name. Typing @assistant or @my finds your own assistant, whatever it is called.
  • Same name as a teammate or a team agent? The purple mark and "Your assistant" tell them apart. Renaming can warn when the name is already used.

Where it works, and who receives the message

Where you askThe card saysWhat happens on Send
A channelWill be sent as you in #salesPosted in that channel as your message.
A reply threadWill be sent as you in this threadPosted as a reply in that thread only.
A chat with a personWill be sent as you to KarthikKarthik reads it as written by you.
A chat with a team agentWill be sent as you to IrisIris takes it as your request and answers you as usual.
Your assistant's own chatNo cardYou are already talking to it. Nothing changes there.

A draft is tied to the conversation where you asked. It cannot be sent anywhere else.

Five rules that never bend

  • Your instruction is never posted. Only you and your assistant see what you asked.
  • Nothing is sent until you press Send. One draft, one decision, every time.
  • Words don't give permission to send. "@Tara handle this" still gives a draft. Replying without review stays the separate Let Tara take over action.
  • You see what it used. Tara can read your private data, so the card shows its sources before the text goes to other people.
  • It is your message. Sent under your name, marked for you alone, recorded in the Audit log.

Underneath, it is the request Cuckoo already has for drafts (conversation, thread and instruction). The new work is the message box mode, the card, and the server rule that refuses to post an assistant mention.

What was built, and what was not

  • Built: the card above the message box. The box stays free while the assistant works. This replaces writing the draft into the box.
  • Built: "respond" answers the newest message that mentions you. The card quotes the message it answers.
  • Built: the button beside Attach stays as a shortcut that only puts the mention in the box.
  • Not built: "What Tara used". The card shows what you asked, not the sources. The assistant cannot report them yet.
  • Not built: the step it is on. While it writes, the card says only that it is writing, not "Reading the sales sheet".
  • Checked without a real model. The flow was tested with a scripted reply, so the quality of real drafts is unproven.
