cuckoo

The phone app, in one page

The same calm, graphite-on-white look as the web app, rebuilt for a thumb. Everything here is a native screen; anything not shown opens the web app.

1Grey outside, white inside. Like the web rail, the tab bar sits on the grey canvas; every screen's content is the white surface.
2One thumb. Main actions live at the bottom: tab bar, composer, Approve. Every target is at least 44 px.
3Sheets, not new pages. Approvals, questions, attachments and filters slide up from the bottom and keep your place in the chat.
4Agents look like teammates. Same round faces as people, told apart only by the small AI tag.
5Nothing private on the lock screen. Notifications say who and what kind, never the message.

Navigation: five tabs

Home

Your day: business clock, what needs you, agents working now and your saved panels. The app opens here.

Chats

Direct messages with agents and people, then channels, pinned at the top.

Needs me

Approvals, questions and finished work waiting for you. The red count is things only you can unblock.

Agents

Every agent you may use. Tap one to see what it does and start a chat.

You

Profile, status, notifications, appearance, devices, workspace switching and sign out.

Opens the web app instead

Creating and editing agents, Settings and People, company rules, Apps and Home panels, Library management, watching or taking over an agent's computer.
