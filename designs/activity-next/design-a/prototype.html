How Design A works

Try these, in order

  1. Press J and K to move down and up the list. The right side changes; the list stays.
  2. On “Approve a 12% discount”, press A. The item leaves, the count drops, the next one opens, and a line at the bottom says what happened.
  3. Open “3 × Refund order”. Reject one, then press “Approve all 2”.
  4. Open “Which courier…”. Send answer stays off until you pick one.
  5. Press “New request arrives” below. It joins the list quietly and your place does not move.
  6. Open “Decided today” at the bottom of the list to see what you did.
  7. Go to Work, pick Nova, and press a row under “Needs you”. It takes you to that exact item.
  8. Go to Planned and pause “Bank reconciliation”, then Undo.
  9. Clear everything in Needs you to see the empty state. “Start over” brings it back.

The layout

Every tab that has a list uses the same two parts: the list on the left, the thing you picked on the right. Needs you, Work and History all behave this way, so you learn it once. Nothing opens in a dialog and nothing dims the list.

The list

  • Three groups, by what you must do: Decide (approve or reject), Answer (give a fact), Fix (something is broken or late).
  • One count. The number on the tab and on the rail is every item in the three groups. “3 × Refund order” counts as 3.
  • Order. Items an admin can already step in on come first, then the longest waiting. Fix items are ordered by how much they block.
  • Rows have no buttons. The title gets the full width. A dot and bold text mean you have not opened it yet.
  • Rows hold still. A new request joins its group; it never moves the row you are on.

The right side

  • Top to bottom it answers: what state is it in, what is being asked, who asked, the exact fields, why it needs you, and what the agent did so far.
  • The buttons are fixed at the bottom, so they are in the same place for every item.
  • The first button is always the usual choice. Each item type has its own: Approve this action, Send answer, Take over the browser, Reconnect, Mark reviewed, Save answer, Mark done.
  • A button that cannot work yet is off, with the reason next to it.

After you decide

Approvals are final in Cuckoo: the first decision counts. So there is no Undo. Instead the decision is written to History at once, a line at the bottom links to it, and “Decided today” keeps it in the list where you can re-read it.

The other tabs

  • Work is today’s Now and Tasks. Agents are flat rows; an agent’s state comes from the same list as Needs you, so approving something there changes it here.
  • Planned is today’s Upcoming, starting at a “Now” line.
  • History opens on the log. The totals are one line, and “6 need a look” is a filter you can press.
  • Insights is today’s Review plus the charts from History. It sits apart because it is read weekly, not hourly.
  • Pause all agents moved into the ⋯ menu on Work. It still asks first.
  • My agents / All agents shows only on the tabs it changes.

On a phone

Make this window narrow to see it. The list fills the screen. Tapping a row opens it full screen with the buttons at the bottom and a Back link at the top. After a decision the next item opens; after the last one you return to the list.

Pause all agents?

Every agent stops at once. Requests that are running or waiting will be stopped and won’t restart on their own.

Until an admin resumes, no agent takes new requests, schedules skip their runs and events from connected tools are not handled.
