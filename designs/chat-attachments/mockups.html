Pictures, files and voice in a conversation

Each kind of attachment gets its own shape, and all three share one width, so a conversation that mixes them keeps one edge. Every proposed screen uses the white raised bubble on the conversation surface. Voice players on this page can be played.

A picture is the message

  • Nothing under it. Name, size and download are already in the image viewer's bar.
  • 320 px on its long side, filling the bubble to its corners.
  • The time sits on the picture. With text, the text goes under the picture and the time under the text.
  • Several pictures share a grid. "+3" opens the viewer at that picture.

Needs from the server: previews of 640 px instead of 256 px, and each picture's width and height so its space is held before it loads.

A file is one row

  • A type tile says what it is before the name is read: PDF, DOC, XLS, and grey for anything else.
  • Name, then type and size. A long name loses its middle, so the ending stays readable.
  • The whole row opens the file. Download appears on hover or focus, and always on touch.
  • One width for every row, so two files in a message line up.
  • No box inside the bubble. The tile carries the structure.

Needs nothing from the server. A first-page preview for PDFs is left out: it would mean rendering PDFs on the server.

Both themes, and a phone

The same parts on the light and dark surfaces. Type colours lighten in dark so the tile still reads. At 320 px every part shrinks with the bubble; nothing scrolls sideways.

Three choices for you

  1. Pictures to the bubble's corners, or inside a thin white frame. Shown: to the corners. A 3 px frame would keep every bubble looking alike, at the cost of a smaller picture.
  2. Coloured type tiles, or grey for all. Shown: coloured. Grey is quieter and matches the rest of Chat; colour is faster to scan in a list of files.
  3. The speed chip on voice messages. Shown. It is the only new control here; leaving it out keeps the player exactly as it is today.
